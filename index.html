About me

My first real lessons in sales came from fine dining. Serving guests at Meadowbrook Country Club, I learned to read a table, anticipate what people need before they ask, and keep my cool when an event grows from an executive dinner to a room of 400 guests. That's consultative selling, even if nobody calls it that.

Now I do the same thing with data. My outlook is that good data is how a team listens at scale. A clean CRM tells a rep who needs a call, tells a leader where the pipeline is thin, and keeps a customer from falling through the cracks when someone leaves the company. I want a career building the systems that make good selling easier.

I'm a Michigan State University senior with a 3.793 GPA and a spot on the Dean's List every semester. I also sought out executive-level sales meetings during my internship, because I wanted to understand the deals behind the data, not just the data.
